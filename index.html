Upload a CSV exported from your bank or payment provider. Files are processed locally in this browser; this demo does not upload them to a server.

Expected CSV columns

Use a header row. Common column names are recognized automatically.

Date, Description, Amount, Type, Category, Reference

Amounts may be signed (negative = expense) or paired with a Type column containing debit/credit. Review your imported transactions for accuracy.

Limitations & privacy

Only selected payment-app notifications are inspected. This does not read UPI apps directly, provide complete bank history, or guarantee that every payment appears. Notification text is sensitive; keep device access protected and disable this permission whenever you want.

✓ Transactions are stored in this browser's local storage.

! Browser storage is not encrypted vault storage. Avoid shared/public devices.
